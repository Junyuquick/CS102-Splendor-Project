File Coverage
-------------

MoveValidator.java
  central entry point for validation
  dispatches by concrete Move subtype

AvailableMovesInspector.java
  answers whether fallback alternatives exist
  used by TakeDifferentMoveRule

BuyMoveRule.java
  validates card purchase source, affordability, payment correctness

ReserveMoveRule.java
  validates reserve source and reserved-card limit

TakeDifferentMoveRule.java
  validates different-token take and fallback special case

TakeSameMoveRule.java
  validates two-of-a-kind take

ReturnTokensMoveRule.java
  validates voluntary return-token turns

PassMoveRule.java
  validates pass turns
