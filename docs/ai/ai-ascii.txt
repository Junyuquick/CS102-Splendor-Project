Shared Dependencies
-------------------

GreedyStrategy
  depends on -> Config, MoveValidator, GameState, Player, Move

AiMoveGenerator
  depends on -> Config, MoveValidator, PaymentCalculator,
                GameState, Player, DevelopmentCard, GemColor, Move

AiMoveScorer
  depends on -> Config, MoveValidator, GameState, Player,
                DevelopmentCard, GemColor, Move


Selection Flow
--------------

GreedyStrategy.chooseMove(...)
   |
   v
chooseEasyMove(...)
   |
   +--> AiMoveGenerator.generateBuyMoves(...)
   +--> AiMoveGenerator.generateReserveMoves(...)
   +--> AiMoveGenerator.generateTakeMoves(...)
   +--> AiMoveGenerator.generateReturnMoves(...)
   |
   +--> weighted category choice
   |    60% prefer take moves
   |    next prefer reserve moves
   |    then buy / fallback / return / pass
   |
   v
returns one legal Move


Generator / Scorer Story
------------------------

AiMoveGenerator
  builds legal candidate lists and filters them through MoveValidator

AiMoveScorer
  scores token-taking moves by how much chosen colors help buy the
  visible market cards

GreedyStrategy
  does not score every move directly; it coordinates the helper classes
  and then picks from the legal candidates
